## Research Paper

**Analysis of Consistency Violating Faults in Distributed Graph Algorithms**  
*By Kamal Giri and Amit Garu*

This paper investigates how **consistency violation faults (CVFs)** affect the performance and stability of **self-stabilizing distributed algorithms**. By analyzing program transitions and rank effects in **Dijkstra’s Token Ring** and **Graph Coloring** algorithms, the study quantifies how individual nodes and their positions influence convergence behavior and fault impact. The results reveal that node degree and positional context play key roles in determining fault sensitivity across distributed systems.
